How endings work: In αγαπάω / μιλάω / ρωτάω, the α of the stem is retained in many everyday forms: αγαπά-ς, αγαπά-με. The shorter αγαπώ, μιλώ, ρωτώ reflect historical vowel contraction. λέω and τρώω have different paradigms; learn them as separate patterns. έχω is another distinct conjugation, not the source of the endings.
